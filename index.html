🧒 My Childhood HTML Experiments

Note: These are just random fun things I coded as a kid. Not to be treated like actual projects. Judge me gently. 😅
And yes—there was no ChatGPT back then, so I had to Google things like a caveman. 🔍🪓
  • Image ↔ Text Interconverter
    During the 2019 CAA protests, the gov pulled the plug on the internet. No WhatsApp, only SMS. I had the genius idea to convert images into text and send them via SMS. Turns out, images are huge and sms can only carry ~900 chars. The plan died as it lived—compressed.
  • UPoE (Universal Program of Everything)
    Tried to simulate a mobile on a PC. It had a calculator, a stopwatch... and big dreams. Then I abandoned it. 😎
  • Shape Draw
    My very early (and very bad) attempt at making something that draws shapes. Don’t expect a masterpiece.
  • Flappy Bird
    My first go at HTML canvas. It's incomplete(walls dont work), and honestly, it's going to stay that way. #nostalgia
  • AbstractArt
    Got curious about HTML animations and played around with decelerating motion. The result? Chaotic beauty (or just chaos).
